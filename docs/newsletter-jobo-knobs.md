# New knobs for old friends

Hi everyone,

If you develop film on a Jobo CPE2 or CPE2+, you probably know this story. A knob
cracks, falls off or simply disappears, and then the hunt begins. Original spare
knobs are almost impossible to find. When one does turn up, it usually costs
far more than a small piece of plastic should, or it comes from a machine that
is in even worse shape than yours.

That used to be the end of the story. Today it doesn't have to be: with a
desktop 3D printer, a replacement knob takes an afternoon and a few grams of
filament.

So I designed a set of them for you.

![Jobo replacement knob with temperature scale](renders/ko-fi/jobo-knob-01-scale-iso.jpg)

## Three faces, one solid knob

Every version shares the same body (Ø33 × 16 mm, 16 grip flutes). Only the
markings on top differ:

- **Temperature scale.** A red zero dot, long and short ticks on every flute,
  and − / + at the ends.
- **Positions.** A red dot with a clear "1" and "2".
- **Pointer.** A red dot and a single indicator line.

The markings are colour inserts, so on a multicolour printer they come out
crisp and inlaid, not painted on. Prefer a single colour? Every design also
comes as a laser-engraving file.

![The three designs from above](renders/ko-fi/jobo-knob-02-scale-top.jpg)

## Built to last, not just to fit

A knob is a small part that gets twisted every day, often with wet hands, next
to warm water and chemistry. So the design is about more than looks:

- **A steel spring clip holds it.** The hub is split, and a standard 15 mm
  internal circlip (DIN 472) sits in a groove around it. It works as a spring:
  it clamps the knob onto the shaft, so the fit stays snug without glue, and
  the knob can still be removed. Circlips cost next to nothing and come in any
  assortment box.
- **Reinforced where it counts.** The root of the hub, where the twisting
  force goes, has chamfers instead of sharp inside corners. The groove for the
  clip has sloped walls, so it prints cleanly with no supports and no fragile
  overhangs.
- **Made for printers, not only for CAD.** The markings are sized for a
  standard 0.4 mm nozzle. Ticks and numbers are wide enough to print as solid
  lines, and everything clears the grip flutes.
- **Checked, not guessed.** The model is fully parametric and was stress-tested
  across sizes. It rebuilds cleanly, and it carries its own sanity checks for
  wall thickness and clearances.

![Underside with the circlip in place](renders/ko-fi/jobo-knob-07-bottom-iso.jpg)

## Materials

- **PETG or ASA** are the best choice. They handle warm water and developer
  chemistry and keep their shape for years.
- **PLA** works, but softens sooner in a warm bath.
- One **DIN 472-15** steel circlip per knob.

## What's in the pack

- **STEP files** ready for Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer: a blank knob, one file
  per design with the colour inserts as separate parts, and a full assembly
  including the circlip.
- **DXF and SVG files** of all markings, for laser engraving a plain knob.
- **Printing notes** for a clean first-layer inlay.
